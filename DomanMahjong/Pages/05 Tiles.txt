TILE VERSATILITY
Useful rule of thumb from the book: suited 3–7 tiles are generally the most versatile for building sequences, followed by 2/8, then 1/9; honors cannot form sequences. A side-wait protorun is usually more flexible than a closed wait, which is usually more flexible than an edge wait.
