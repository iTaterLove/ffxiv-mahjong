HOW TO USE THIS SECTION
A legal winning hand must have a valid shape and at least one yaku. Dora, red dora, ura-dora, and kan-dora add bonus han but are not yaku. Yaku values and some edge-case interpretations can vary by ruleset; use the event's official list as the final authority.

Notation:
- Closed han: value when the hand remains closed.
- Open han: value after opening the hand, if the yaku is still valid.
- “Closed only” means the yaku cannot be earned with an open hand.
- Values below describe common four-player riichi rules and should be verified for a specific ruleset.

YAKUMAN: LIMIT HANDS
Yakuman are top-limit hands. Rules vary on double yakuman, multiple yakuman, and certain responsibility-payment cases.

| Yakuman | Basic condition |
| --- | --- |
| Kokushi Musou (Thirteen Orphans) | One each of the 13 terminal/honor types plus a duplicate of any one of them. |
| Suuankou (Four Concealed Triplets) | Four concealed triplets/quads and a pair; winning method and wait can affect double-yakuman treatment. |
| Daisangen (Big Three Dragons) | Triplets/quads of all three dragons. |
| Shousuushii (Little Four Winds) | Three wind triplets/quads and a pair of the fourth wind. |
| Daisuushii (Big Four Winds) | Triplets/quads of all four winds; often treated as double yakuman in some rulesets. |
| Tsuuiisou (All Honors) | Every tile is an honor. |
| Chinroutou (All Terminals) | Every tile is a suited terminal (1 or 9). |
| Ryuuiisou (All Green) | Every tile is one of the tiles recognized as green by the ruleset. |
| Chuuren Poutou (Nine Gates) | A qualifying one-suit hand with the required 1112345678999 structure plus one additional tile; pure nine-gates treatment varies. |
| Suukantsu (Four Kans) | Four quads. |
| Tenhou (Blessing of Heaven) | Dealer's qualifying first-draw win before interruption. |
| Chiihou (Blessing of Earth) | Non-dealer's qualifying first-draw win before interruption. |
| Renhou (Blessing of Man) | Early non-dealer ron before the first uninterrupted draw cycle; optional and varies substantially by ruleset. |

SITUATIONAL AND COMBINED YAKU NOTES
- Haitei and Houtei refer to the last live-wall draw and its resulting last discard, respectively; do not confuse them.
- Rinshan Kaihou is associated with a replacement draw after a kan.
- Chankan is a ron on a kan declaration's tile when the rules permit it.
- Yakuhai may stack: a dragon triplet is one han; a seat-wind triplet is one han; a round-wind triplet is one han. If a wind is both seat and round wind, it can score for both under common rules.
- A yaku can combine with other yaku, but some combinations are structurally incompatible.
- A hand is scored using the applicable interpretation and highest legal scoring result under the rules.

YAKU CHECKLIST BEFORE WINNING
1. Is the tile shape complete?
2. Is the hand legal under the current rules?
3. Which yaku are present, and what are their han values?
4. Which dora indicators and red tiles apply?
5. Is the hand furiten for ron?
6. What fu and payments apply?
